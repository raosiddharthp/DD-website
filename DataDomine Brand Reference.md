# DataDomine Brand Reference

## Company
DataDomine — technical practitioner education for enterprise architects, 
solution architects, and ML engineers in regulated environments.

## Tagline
Production-Ready. By Design.

## Philosophy
Every decision a technical practitioner makes should be informed by data. 
DataDomine trains practitioners to make decisions the way the job actually 
demands — from data, under constraint, with the rigour that regulated 
production environments require.

## The Clarity Point Framework
DataDomine's proprietary pedagogical framework. Ten lenses applied to 
every programme:
1. The Production Ready Standard
2. The Mario Journey (greenfield, brownfield, integration contexts)
3. Discovery over instruction
4. Jungian behavioural discipline
5. Economic decisions first
6. Agile value flow
7. Data foundation — all decisions informed by data
8. Failure modes taxonomy
9. The handoff discipline
10. The restraint layer (the unbuilt thing)

## Brand Voice
Precise. Direct. Invested.
Not: inspirational, motivational, hype-driven, platform-marketing.
British English throughout.
No bullet points in hero or problem sections — prose only.
No stock photography references.
Never use: passionate, journey, transform, unlock, empower.
