The Framework
The Clarity Point Framework

Every DataDomine programme is built on a single observation: every practitioner has experienced the moment when a complex decision resolves into something they can act on and defend. The Clarity Point Framework is built to design those moments deliberately, repeatedly, and at every level of the work. It applies ten lenses to every programme — from economic judgment to behavioural discipline to the governance of failure.

  • Economic Decisions First. Architectural decisions are economic decisions wearing a technical coat.
  • The Behavioural Layer. The behaviour of the practitioner in the rooms where approval is sought determines whether correct decisions get built.
  • Discovery Over Instruction. What is discovered is owned. What is told is forgotten.
  • The Failure Modes Taxonomy. Name the failure modes specific to your context before they occur — and instrument the system to detect them early.
  • The Handoff Discipline. A system that cannot be handed off cleanly is not production-ready.
