Not Training. Facilitated Design.

A DataDomine workshop is not a course. There is no curriculum, no cohort, and no fixed outcome. The facilitator brings a structured method and the discipline to hold a room to it. The participants bring the real problem. What the engagement produces is determined by what the work demands — a defensible decision, a resolved conflict, or a documented capability gap — not by what a programme catalogue says it should.

Three Triggers
  • The High-Stakes Decision A consequential architectural decision is approaching — a platform choice, a governance model, a system boundary — and the team cannot afford to get it wrong. The workshop produces a documented, defensible ADR with the constraint map that justifies it.
  • The Stuck Team A cross-functional team is misaligned — on direction, on priorities, or on the nature of the problem itself. The workshop surfaces the conflict, structures the disagreement, and produces a shared set of decisions the team can move on.
  • The Capability Gap A practice is scaling and the gap between where practitioners are and where the work requires them to be is becoming visible. The workshop produces a written assessment and a development path the practice lead can act on.
What Every Engagement Includes
  • Discovery conversation at no charge
  • Written brief confirmed before the workshop
  • Post-workshop artefact document within 5 working days
  • Thirty-minute follow-up call at four weeks
