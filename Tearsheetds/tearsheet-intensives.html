What A Live Intensive Is

Three hours. One decision. Real working time. Not a webinar — a structured working session with a defined output that participants leave with. The topic is specific, the method is precise, and the time is used. There is no padding, no motivational framing, and no content that could have been a document.

Every session is built around a single lens from the Clarity Point Framework, applied to a problem class the participant recognises from their own work.

Who These Are For

Individual practitioners. Open enrolment. No company approval required, no cohort commitment, no prerequisite. Pay and attend. The format is designed for the practitioner who needs to move on a specific decision or capability now, not at the next budget cycle.
