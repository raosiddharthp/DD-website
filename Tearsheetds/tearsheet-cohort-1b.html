The Problem
Models are not systems.

Most ML programmes teach practitioners how to build models. Almost none teach them how to build the systems around the model — the governance layer, the audit trail, the retraining pipeline, the compliance documentation — that make those models viable in a regulated production environment.

The result is a practitioner who can train and evaluate a model but cannot answer the questions that matter to a risk committee, a compliance officer, or a regulated board: How does this decision get explained? How is drift detected and governed? Who owns the retraining trigger? What is the audit trail if a prediction is challenged?

These are not peripheral concerns. In regulated environments they are the difference between a model that reaches production and one that does not.

Who This Is For

ML Engineers, Data Scientists, and Senior Developers building ML systems in finance, healthcare, supply chain, insurance, or any regulated environment where models must survive a compliance audit, pass a risk review, and sustain a governed production lifecycle.

What You Leave With

A complete ML system artefact — a portfolio piece you can present to a governance board, a risk committee, or a hiring manager.
