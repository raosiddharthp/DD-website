Programmes

The Programmes.

DataDomine offers four distinct engagement types. Each is designed for a specific problem, a specific audience, and a specific moment in a practitioner's career or an organisation's capability journey. Two cohort programmes built around a shared spine project and defended at a capstone. A facilitated workshop format for high-stakes decisions and stuck teams. And a library of single-session intensives for practitioners who need to move on a specific decision or capability now, without a cohort commitment and without waiting for the next budget cycle.

Enterprise AI Architecture Cohort

ML Engineering for Regulated Industries

Architecture Design Workshop
